### 🔭 Current Research Focus

My current work revolves around lightweight mapping and real-time trajectory generation for UAVs:

* **⚡ Lightweight Neural Mapping & Planning**
    * Developed a lightweight neural network for real-time occupancy status mapping within occupancy grids, serving as the foundation for efficient motion planning.
* **🚁 Swarm Trajectory Optimization (MINCO & Diffeomorphism)**
    * Proposed a 5-DOF UAV swarm trajectory optimization algorithm based on the MINCO framework and diffeomorphic mapping.
    * **Key Tech:** Derived full-system analytical gradients and utilized L-BFGS for solving unconstrained optimization problems.
    * **Impact:** Reduced planning time to **milliseconds**, strictly ensuring dynamic feasibility and geometric safety constraints for agile flight in complex environments.

### 🧠 Future Research Interests

My vision is to evolve robots from "Reactive" to **"Cognitive & Safe"** — endowing them with a "Prefrontal Cortex" for planning and a "Cerebellum" for safety reflexes.

* **🌪️ Agile Perception-Action Loops (RL)**
    * Leveraging Reinforcement Learning to fine-tune large Vision Models (e.g., ViT), breaking the performance ceiling of imitation learning for limit-pushing agility.
* **🌍 Predictive Latent World Models**
    * Transitioning from Model-Free to **Model-Based**: Building lightweight world models to "imagine" dynamic obstacle trajectories in latent space for predictive planning.
* **🛡️ Neuro-Symbolic Safety Guarantees**
    * Addressing the "Black Box" trust issue by learning **Neural Control Barrier Functions (Neural CBF)** from high-dim inputs, embedding them as hard constraints in MPC/RL for provable safety.
